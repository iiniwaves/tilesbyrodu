About Tiles by Rudu

Finishing spaces with materials that last

We supply tiles and building finishes to homeowners, contractors and developers who want a space that looks considered and holds up over time.

Our Story

Started with a simple standard: no shortcuts on material

Tiles by Rudu began as a response to a common frustration — tiles that looked good in the showroom but chipped, faded or cracked within a year of installation. We set out to supply materials that meet a higher bar, sourced specifically for consistency in finish and long-term durability.

Today, we work with individual homeowners renovating a single bathroom as readily as we work with contractors finishing multi-unit developments, supplying everything from floor and wall tiles to paving blocks and interlocking stones.

Our Mission

To make premium tiles and building finishes accessible, dependable and easy to order — so every project, large or small, gets materials it can be built on.

Our Vision

To be the trusted first call for tiles and finishing materials in every project we're part of, known for consistency as much as for style.
